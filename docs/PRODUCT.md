# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS, single file, published as a shareable link (MJ chose "HTML 한 장, 링크 공유"). Phone-first layout; on desktop the app column sits beside a brand/intro panel (reference: vurity.kr). Home-screen install guidance like a web app (reference: wecosmo.space "앱 설치").

## Users

- Primary (demand): beginners about to start a life situation or hobby — first target is university freshmen ("새내기"), then swim/run/golf/fishing beginners and office workers. They don't know what to buy, follow ads, and end up with unused items.
- Supply: ordinary "고수" (experts) — club captains, 학교생활 만렙 students, 10-year office workers, hobby veterans. Not celebrities. They want their setup recognized and their know-how kept.
- Situation: arrive on a phone from an Instagram post or an Everytime post link; skim one person's bag; decide what to buy, what to skip.

## Product Purpose

Open a real expert's bag and show, item by item, what a beginner should buy first, what they don't need, and the cheapest sensible way to get it (new / used / free share). Success in round 1: seed bags collected, beginners save and follow bags, recorded weekly (outreach response rate, bag submission rate, clicks by channel).

## Positioning

"쇼핑몰은 사라고만 하지만, 고수는 '이건 사지 마'도 알려줍니다." The bag of an ordinary expert, with an explicit "don't buy this" and new/used/share price comparison on the same card. Commerce sites only push buying; YouTube what's-in-my-bag is creator video and celebrity-led.

## Operating Context

- Entry from Instagram (discovery) and Everytime (school belonging, beginner demand). Mobile.
- Content is concierge-made in round 1: the founder interviews the expert, photographs the bag (flat lay), and builds the card; expert only confirms (~20 min photo + 20 min Q&A).
- Card fields: situation + total budget title; full bag photo; numbered pins (≤8 items) with one-line tip (≤40 chars); per-item status (in use / willing to sell used / free share); source badge (bought myself / used / handed down / sponsored / affiliate link); substitute badge ("no need to buy — use X at home"); mandatory "don't buy this" item; best item / won't rebuy; 3-way price comparison (all new vs expert combo) with price date.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Round 1: no native app, no login, no real transactions. Prototype and seed content only.
- Main situations limited to 6–7: 대학생, 직장인, 여행, 수영, 러닝, 골프, 낚시. University freshman first.
- Later stages (not now): own price-comparison engine (new+used+share), category expansion, photo auto-classification.
- Undecided: service name. Candidates 갓템(GOTEM) / 갓템(GOATEM) / 고수템(GOSUTEM); trademark not checked. Mockup uses a provisional name.
- Prices in mockup are illustrative examples, must be labeled as examples.

## Brand Commitments

- No voyeuristic wording ("염탐", "훔쳐보다"). Sharing and pride, not peeking.
- Not a women-only beauty app; men and women, hobbies. Cosmetics are one category, not the frame.
- No celebrities. Ordinary experts.
- Gen-Z hip but readable for middle-aged judges: define coined terms.
- Must not feel like a shopping mall pushing discounts.

## Evidence on Hand

- KWDI issue paper (2026-01-31): university students' top-3 frequently used SNS — Instagram 81.1%, YouTube 71.2%, KakaoTalk 70.3%, Everytime 9.1% (1–3rd rank response rate).
- KCCI survey (2025-04 report): 75.3% of recent used-goods traders positive about used trading.
- No real seed bags yet, no testimonials, no user counts. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. The bag is the content; commerce follows from browsing, never leads.
2. Always show what NOT to buy alongside what to buy.
3. Make commercial interest visible (sponsored / affiliate badges) instead of hiding it.
4. Tiny ask for experts, lasting reward (their bag becomes a profile page).
5. Beginner-first language: situation and budget before product names.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable by middle-aged mentors/judges: base text ≥16px on mobile, coined terms explained once, sufficient contrast.
